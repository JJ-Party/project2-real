Melee Class

This terraria class is the most tanky; it provides great defense, allowing you to tank hits from powerful bosses, at the cost of low range. Melee weapons include: Swords, maces / flails, and yoyos.

Ranger Class

This terraria class is the most consistent; you can reliably deal a good deal of damage, while keeping a safe distance. Ranger weapons include: bows, guns, and other various ranged weapons.

Mage Class

This terraria class is the most versatile; you can choose between many different magic weapons, from shooting meteors to water, to shooting tracking star projectiles. Mage weapons include: staves, wands, spell books, and other various magic items.

Summoner Class

This terraria class is the most evasive; you can reliably let your minions do all the work for you, so you only have to deal with dodging boss attacks with low defense armor, as they won't target your summons, but will target you. Summoner weapons include: whips, summon staves (minions), and sentries.
